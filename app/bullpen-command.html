How the score works

Miss distance is measured in inches from the target to where the ball is caught, using the 17-inch plate width from your zone box as the ruler. A pitch within a third of the hit radius scores 100. The score falls in a straight line to 0 at three times the hit radius. With the default 6" radius, a 6" miss scores 75 and anything 18" or more scores 0. The command score is the average across all tagged pitches, and the grade puts it on the 20–80 scouting scale.
